ABOUT

Practical technology for Québec operations.

Delta Operations helps small businesses simplify work, connect systems, and apply automation or AI where it is justified.

Operations first

We begin with the people, work, exceptions, and desired result—not a preferred technology.

Custom where useful

Existing software is recommended when it fits. Custom systems are built when the workflow genuinely requires them.

Measurable improvement

Time, volume, delay, quality, and adoption measures make the result visible.

Bilingual service

Discovery, documentation, interfaces, and support can be delivered in French and English.

Responsible AI

AI is not appropriate for every task. Privacy, source quality, human review, and failure modes are addressed explicitly.

Honest limits

No automation eliminates every exception. Good systems make uncertainty visible and provide a safe recovery path.
