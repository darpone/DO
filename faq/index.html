FAQ

Straight answers before you start.

Common questions from Québec small businesses evaluating operational improvement.

What does a project cost?

Cost depends on scope, systems, data quality, risk, and the level of change required. A short audit is used to define options and an estimate before a build commitment.

How long does a project take?

A focused diagnostic can take days or weeks; implementation may range from a small prototype to a phased program. The timeline is confirmed after scope and dependencies are understood.

What happens in a process audit?

We map the workflow, interview the people involved, identify volume and exceptions, review existing tools, and prioritize opportunities by value, effort, and risk.

How is data protected?

The design should minimize collection, restrict access, document data flow, and use appropriate provider controls. Requirements are assessed for each project; highly sensitive data needs additional review.

Will artificial intelligence replace employees?

The goal is to reduce repetitive work and support better decisions. AI should retain human review where errors, ambiguity, privacy, or accountability matter.

Can you integrate our existing software?

Often, yes. Feasibility depends on APIs, exports, permissions, licensing, and data quality. These constraints are checked before an integration is promised.

What support is available after launch?

Support can include documentation, training, monitoring, issue response, and measured optimization based on the agreed engagement.

Who owns a custom system?

Ownership, licenses, credentials, hosting, and third-party components should be defined explicitly in the project agreement before work begins.

Do you work with Québec SMEs in French and English?

Yes. Discovery, documentation, interfaces, and support can be delivered in French or English for organizations across Québec.
