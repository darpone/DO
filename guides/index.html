GUIDES

Practical guidance for better preparation.

Short guides that complement the interactive tools.

Prepare for a process audit

Choose one specific process, define its start and end, involve two or three people who know it, and bring real volume, delay, error, and example data.

Assess an automation

Check frequency, rules, input quality, exceptions, systems, ownership, and baseline measures before selecting a tool.

Prepare the data

Name the system of record, define important fields, find duplicates, and confirm access and retention rules.

Plan adoption

Involve users early, test with real cases, document manual recovery, and measure use after launch.
