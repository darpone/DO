Privacy

A clear explanation of information processed by this website.

In short: no accounts, advertising cookies, or analytics. Tools stay local; the checklist uses local storage for progress.

Information is used to respond and retained as needed for applicable obligations. For access, correction, or deletion, email contact@deltaoperations.ca.
