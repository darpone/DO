CASE STUDIES

Representative scenarios, presented honestly.

These examples show how a solution could be designed. They do not claim to represent real clients.

Representative scenario

Request intake for a service firm

One structured entry point validates fields, creates the file, assigns an owner, and confirms receipt.

This illustrates a possible architecture; it is not a named client or measured result.

Representative scenario

Quoting for a trades business

Scope data is collected once, pricing rules are applied, and the quote moves through approval and follow-up.

Savings depend on volume, exceptions, and existing systems.

Representative scenario

Operating reports for a distributor

Validated data feeds a scheduled exception-oriented report covering shortages, variance, and required action.

No real financial outcome is claimed.
