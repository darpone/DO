Notice

Privacy

A clear explanation of the information this website processes.

In short: no accounts, advertising cookies, or analytics tracking. The tools run locally. The contact form transmits only what you choose to send.

Information collected

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, optional organization name, message, and consent.

Interactive tools

The diagnostic, ROI calculator, and requirements brief generator run in your browser. Their answers are not sent to Delta Operations. Your language choice may be kept in browser local storage to simplify your next visit.

Use

Contact-form information is used only to respond to your request, understand your need, and communicate about relevant services. It is not sold.

Form provider

The form is processed by Formspree, an external provider. You may avoid this processing by emailing contact@deltaoperations.ca directly.

Retention and requests

Information is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion, email contact@deltaoperations.ca.

Security and limits

Do not submit secrets, passwords, financial information, medical information, or other highly sensitive information through the form. No transmission method can be guaranteed absolutely secure.
