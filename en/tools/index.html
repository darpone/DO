Tool 01

Workflow diagnostic

Assess one specific process. For each statement, choose from low (0) to high (4).

1. The process is performed frequently.
2. It requires substantial manual time.
3. The same data is entered in multiple systems.
4. The process has handoffs or follow-ups between people.
5. Inputs follow a reasonably consistent format.
6. Errors or omissions lead to rework.
7. Decision rules are generally explicit.
8. A process owner can participate in the project.
9. Current volume or effort can be measured.
10. Current delays affect customers, revenue, or capacity.
11. The systems involved support exports or integrations.
0/100

Three recommendations

    This is an indicative diagnostic. A real assessment must also consider exceptions, security, data quality, and organizational change.
